## Speak instead of typing

Some text boxes have a **microphone** button: [Ask VoiceDesk](/t/:tenant/ask), the reply box in the [Inbox](/t/:tenant/inbox), search (Ctrl+K), and the answer boxes under Questions to answer on [Knowledge](/t/:tenant/knowledge).

1. Click the microphone. The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone: choose **Allow**.
2. Speak. Your words appear in the box as you go, in your VoiceDesk language.
3. Stop talking for a moment, or click the microphone again, and it stops. It also stops after a minute.
4. Check the words, change anything you need, then send as usual. Nothing is sent until you press the button.

## Switching it off

- **Just for you:** on your [Account](/t/:tenant/account) page, untick **Show the microphone in text fields**.
- **For everyone in the business** (owners and admins): [Assistant settings](/t/:tenant/agent) → **Workspace** → untick **Voice typing in text fields**.

## Why is there no microphone?

- Someone switched it off, for you or for the business (see above).
- The browser can't use a microphone: it needs a secure address (https) and a recent browser.
- Your business has used this month's AI allowance. Voice typing uses a tiny part of it (about a penny a minute).
- It isn't set up on this VoiceDesk yet (Platform admin → Setup → Voice typing).

## Privacy

Your voice goes straight from your browser to Deepgram, the same speech service that answers calls, using a key that lasts 30 seconds. VoiceDesk keeps no recording and no transcript: the words only go into the box.

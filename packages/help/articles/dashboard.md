## What the Dashboard shows

The [Dashboard](/t/:tenant) is the first page you see for a business. It sums up every conversation the assistant and your team had with customers, across all channels. Times are shown in your business's time zone.

## Choosing a period

Use the switch at the top right:

- **Daily** shows the last 14 days.
- **Weekly** shows the last 12 weeks.
- **Monthly** shows the last 12 months.

Each number is compared with the period before (for example "vs the 14 days before"), so you can see whether things are going up or down.

## The key numbers

- **Conversations**: every call, chat, WhatsApp, email and form enquiry.
- **Customers**: known customers who got in touch.
- **Bookings made**.
- **Satisfied**: the share of ratings that were good, from customer feedback.
- **Handled by the assistant**: conversations no person had to step into.
- **Voice response time**: the median time from the end of a caller's speech to the assistant's reply.
- **Taken by your team**: calls answered in the VA console, and how many were missed.
- **Tickets & messages**: complaints and messages for the team.

## Your assistant this month

This card adds up what your assistant has done since the 1st of the month:

- conversations handled;
- calls answered while you were closed (it needs opening hours in your [business profile](/t/:tenant/profile));
- bookings it made;
- messages it took;
- an **estimated value**.

The value is the number of bookings × your average booking value. Set that under [Assistant settings](/t/:tenant/agent) → **Reports**. Owners and admins also get this as an email on the 1st of each month.

## The charts

- **Engagement by touchpoint**: conversations per channel (phone calls, calls by the assistant, website chat, WhatsApp, email and forms), plus the **Channel mix**.
- **From conversation to result**: how many conversations led to a booking, ticket or message.
- **How customers felt**: positive, neutral, negative or very unhappy.
- **What they wanted**: questions, bookings, complaints, sign-ups and so on.
- **When customers get in touch**: darker squares mean more conversations at that hour. The busiest time is shown underneath.
- **Satisfaction over time**, **Voice response time** and **Bookings, tickets and messages** trends.

## Also in this period

This box flags things worth acting on:

- **Missed handoffs**: callers nobody at the console could take.
- **Unanswered questions**: things to add to your business info. Fix them from [Records](/t/:tenant/records).
- **Calls by the assistant**: answered and placed outbound calls.

## Business details and Recent activity

**Business details** shows your country, time zone, main language, **Data region** and **Status** (Setting up, Live or Suspended).

**Recent activity** lists important changes, such as someone signing in, a teammate joining or the business profile being approved. Only owners and admins see it.

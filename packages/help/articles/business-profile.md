## Why the profile matters

The [Business profile](/t/:tenant/profile) holds the facts your assistant can state with confidence: opening hours, services and prices, address, policies and so on. **Only the approved version is used with customers.** If the page says "Not approved yet — the assistant has no business facts", the assistant can't tell customers anything reliable about your business.

Owners and admins can edit and approve the profile. Staff can view it.

## Generating a draft

1. Add your website and documents on [Knowledge](/t/:tenant/knowledge) first.
2. Click **Generate from knowledge**. It takes about a minute.
3. The draft is marked **AI-generated, please check**.

You can also fill in the profile by hand without generating.

## Checking and approving

1. Go through every section and correct anything wrong or missing. Sections that differ from the live version are marked **Differs from live**.
2. Click **Save draft** to keep your work without publishing.
3. When you're happy, click **Approve & publish**. You'll see "Approved. The assistant now uses this profile."

The top of the page shows which version is live and which draft you're editing.

## What's in the profile

- **Basics**: **Business name** and **What the business does**.
- **Opening hours** for each day (tick **Closed** for days off), plus **Notes (bank holidays, seasonal hours…)**.
- **Services and prices**: each **Service** with its **Price (as published)**, length in **Minutes**, whether it's **Bookable**, and a **Description**.
- **Location and contact**: **Address**, **Area**, **Directions**, **Parking**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Website**.
- **Booking rules**: **Minimum notice (hours)**, **Gap between appointments (minutes)** and **Cancellation policy**.
- **Policies**: any **Topic** and its **Details**.
- **FAQs**: questions and answers the assistant can use word for word.
- **Staff**: names and roles.

## The sales section

**Sales: turning enquiries into customers** lets the assistant finish conversations on its own:

- **What should a successful conversation end in?** A booking, a sign-up, or either.
- **Who you serve (ages, eligibility, customer types)**.
- **Why customers choose you**.
- **Current offers**, with an optional end date.
- **How a new customer signs up / enrols** and an optional **Sign-up link**.

## Keep it current

Every time something changes, edit the profile and approve it again. Check [Records](/t/:tenant/records) for **Questions the assistant couldn't answer** and add those answers as FAQs.

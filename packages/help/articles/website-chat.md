## What Website chat does

[Website chat](/t/:tenant/website-chat) puts your assistant on your website as a chat button. Visitors chat with it just like on the phone: it answers questions, books, takes messages and signs people up. Conversations appear in the [Inbox](/t/:tenant/inbox), where you can take over.

Only owners and admins can change these settings.

## Turning it on

Under **Chat on your website**, click **Turn on**. While it's off, the chat button doesn't appear, even if the snippet is on your site.

## Where it can appear

In **Your websites**, list every website the chat may appear on, one per line (for example https://www.example.co.uk). Only these websites can show your chat, which stops other sites copying your snippet. The chat window carries a short-lived security code, so copying the chat's address doesn't work either.

When VoiceDesk's bot check is switched on, the chat quietly checks that the visitor is a person before it starts. Most people never see it; occasionally a visitor is asked to tick a box.

## How it looks

- **Chat title**: the heading at the top of the chat window.
- **Colour**: the button and accent colour.
- **Button position**: **Bottom right** or **Bottom left**.

Click **Save** after any change. Use **Preview** to see it on the page (the chat must be on to preview).

## Adding it to your website

1. Under **Add it to your website**, click **Copy** to copy the snippet.
2. Paste it just before the closing body tag on every page. In most website builders (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress and so on) there's a "custom code" or "footer code" setting for this.
3. Publish your website and refresh it. The chat button should appear in the corner you chose.

## Getting a new key

If your snippet has been copied somewhere it shouldn't be, click **New key**. The current snippet stops working until you replace it on your website with the new one.

## Before VoiceDesk is hosted

While VoiceDesk is running on a single computer rather than a public server, real websites can't reach it, so the chat won't appear for visitors yet. You can still try it with **Preview**. See "Website chat isn't showing" in Help for more checks.

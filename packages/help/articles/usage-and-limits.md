## What this page shows

Open **Usage & limits** in the sidebar (under Settings) to reach [Usage & limits](/t/:tenant/usage). It shows this month's spend in three parts:

- **AI**: the cost of the assistant's answers, summaries, Ask VoiceDesk and the Help assistant.
- **Texts**: confirmation, reminder and alert texts sent through a real phone carrier.
- **Calls**: minutes on the phone (incoming and outgoing) and browser voice calls.

Each part has a progress bar against your monthly limit. Months follow your business's time zone. The amounts are estimates based on VoiceDesk's provider costs.

## Setting your limits

Only the business owner can change the limits.

1. Enter an amount in pounds for **AI**, **Texts** and **Calls**.
2. Click **Save limits**.

You can raise or lower them whenever you like, up to the amount shown under each box. Set a limit to 0 to switch that kind of spending off completely. New businesses start at £20 for AI, £10 for texts and £20 for calls.

## What happens when a limit is reached

- **AI**: the assistant doesn't go silent. It takes messages instead: it asks for the customer's name, number and message, and saves it for your team in Records. Ask VoiceDesk and AI summaries pause too.
- **Texts**: confirmations, reminders and alert texts stop. Emails still go.
- **Calls**: the assistant's callbacks and confirmation calls, and people phoning customers, stop. Incoming calls are still answered, so customers can always reach you.

Owners and admins get an email at 80% and again at 100% of each limit. Everything starts again on the 1st of next month, or straight away if you raise the limit.

## Where calls and texts can go

To protect against fraud, calls and texts only go to allowed countries (the UK by default). Premium-rate and special-rate numbers (for example 09, 087, 084 and 070 numbers) are always blocked. This also applies to the transfer, on-call and alert numbers in [Assistant settings](/t/:tenant/agent).

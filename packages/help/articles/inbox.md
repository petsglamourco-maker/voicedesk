## What the Inbox is for

The [Inbox](/t/:tenant/inbox) collects every text conversation with customers: website chat, WhatsApp, email and forms. The assistant answers them automatically, and you can step in at any time. Phone calls aren't here; they're on [Calls](/t/:tenant/calls).

New messages appear live, without refreshing the page. The notifications bell in the top bar also tells you how many conversations are waiting for a person and how many calls are ringing at the VA console.

## Filters

Use the tabs at the top of the list:

- **All**: every conversation.
- **Unread**: conversations with new messages.
- **Wants a person**: the customer asked for a person, or the assistant needs help. Check this first.
- **Mine**: conversations you've taken over.

## Reading a conversation

Click a conversation to open it. Messages are labelled **Customer**, **Assistant** or **Team**. The side shows the **Contact** and any records created, such as a booking reference.

## Taking over from the assistant

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Take over**, or simply type in the reply box. The box says "Write a reply (this takes over from the assistant)…".
3. Write your message and click **Send**.

While you have the conversation, it shows **A person is replying** and the assistant stays quiet.

## Handing back

When you've finished, click **Hand back to assistant**. The assistant picks up again and knows a person was helping. The conversation then shows **Assistant replying**.

## Approving email and form drafts

For email and form enquiries, the assistant usually writes a draft instead of sending straight away. Drafts are marked **Draft reply, waiting for approval**.

1. Read the draft.
2. Click **Edit** to change it if needed (or **Cancel** to undo your edit).
3. Click **Approve and send**.

If the customer didn't leave an email address, you'll see "This customer didn't leave an email address. Call them back instead."

## Keyboard shortcuts

On a computer, press **J** and **K** to move down and up the list, and **R** to jump to the reply box.

## Who can reply

Owners, admins and staff can all read and reply in the Inbox.

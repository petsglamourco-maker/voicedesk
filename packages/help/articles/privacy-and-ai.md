## The assistant always says it's an AI

Every conversation starts with a fixed greeting that tells the customer they're talking to an AI assistant, for example "Hello, you're through to (your business name). I'm Amelia, the AI assistant here. How can I help you today?" This can't be switched off or reworded, in any language. It's a legal and trust requirement, and outbound calls the assistant makes keep the same disclosure.

## Call recording notice

If **Record calls** is on under [Assistant](/t/:tenant/agent), callers hear a short notice at the start: "Just so you know, this call is recorded for quality and training." Transcripts are always kept. Recordings are stored on VoiceDesk's own storage and are only available to your team on [Calls](/t/:tenant/calls). Keep recordings only as long as you need them.

## Data region

Your business details on the [Dashboard](/t/:tenant) show the **Data region** where your business's data is kept (UK for UK businesses).

## AI providers and DeepSeek

Customer messages are processed by the AI provider your VoiceDesk administrator has switched on. One of the options, DeepSeek, processes data in China. Until a UK GDPR international transfer assessment has been done:

- only use DeepSeek with demo or test data;
- for real customers, the platform owner should switch to another provider (such as Claude) under **Platform admin → AI providers**.

## Marketing and contacting customers

- Marketing is only sent to contacts marked as agreeing to it.
- Only import contacts you have a lawful reason to contact.
- Contacts marked **Don't phone** are never called by the assistant.
- Calls the assistant makes only happen between 8am and 9pm.
- Forms ask for consent before an emailed reply is sent.

## What staff can see

People only see the businesses they belong to, and staff can't change settings. Use two-step verification on [Account](/t/:tenant/account) to protect your sign-in.

## Using Help safely

The Help assistant can't see your customers' data, and it doesn't need to. Don't paste customers' names, phone numbers, emails or other personal details into Help or Contact support. Describe the problem instead, for example "a customer's booking didn't appear in my calendar".

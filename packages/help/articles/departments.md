## What departments do

[Departments & menu](/t/:tenant/departments) lets callers choose who they need, for example "For Sales, press 1. For Support, press 2." Callers always hear the AI greeting first, then the menu. They can press a key, or just say what they need. The assistant then carries on with that department's instructions, and can still book, arrange a call back or take a message.

Every call and chat is tagged with its department. You can filter by department on [Calls](/t/:tenant/calls) and in the Inbox. If a caller changes topic, the assistant moves them to the right department, and the call page shows each move.

Departments are included in the Growth and Pro plans.

## Setting up the menu

1. Click **Add department** and give it a name. Choose its key (1–9). 0 always means "speak to a person".
2. Open **Details** to add:
   - **Instructions for the assistant**, for example "Ask for the order number first."
   - **When a caller asks for a person**: ring this department's people, transfer to a number, or take a message.
   - **Transfer number**: their desk phone or call centre (transfers cost call minutes).
   - **Email for messages**: messages, callback requests and call summaries for this department.
   - **Payment link**: see below.
   - **People in this department**: they're rung for its calls and get its alerts.
   - **Ring the team first**: ring this department's people before the assistant helps.
3. Switch on **Play the menu after the greeting**. Choose **Only during opening hours** if you'd like the assistant to answer directly at night.
4. Click **Save menu**. The preview shows what callers will hear.

Switch on **Offer departments in chat and WhatsApp** to show the same choices as buttons in your website chat and as a numbered list in WhatsApp.

## Payment links

Paste your own secure payment link (from Stripe, SumUp, PayPal or similar) into a department, for example Payments. When a customer wants to pay, the assistant texts or emails them the link after confirming their number or address. The money goes straight to you.

The assistant never takes card details over the phone or in chat.

## Who gets a department's calls and messages

- Calls handed to a person ring only that department's people (if it has any). Otherwise they ring everyone who answers calls.
- Urgent messages, callback requests and call summaries go to the owners and admins as usual. They also go to the department's people and its email address.

## Your plan

Open **Plan & billing** in the sidebar (under Settings) to reach [Plan & billing](/t/:tenant/billing). It shows your plan, what it includes each month (phone minutes, texts and AI), and when it renews.

Your plan decides everything your assistant can do. VoiceDesk doesn't limit anything your plan covers. If you regularly run out, move to a bigger plan.

| Plan    | Price a month (excl. VAT) | Phone minutes | Texts | AI  | Phone numbers | Team members |
| ------- | ------------------------- | ------------- | ----- | --- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Starter | £29                       | 100           | 60    | £4  | 1             | 3            |
| Growth  | £79                       | 350           | 200   | £10 | 1             | 8            |
| Pro     | £219                      | 1,000         | 600   | £25 | 2             | 25           |

Every plan includes website chat, forms, the email inbox, bookings and calendars. Growth and Pro add WhatsApp, virtual assistants and people answering calls, customer feedback surveys, the daily brief, and AI callbacks and confirmation calls.

## The free trial

A new business starts with a 14-day free trial, with no card needed. The trial includes every feature, with small allowances (20 phone minutes, 10 texts and £1 of AI). A banner shows when 3 days are left.

When the trial ends, the assistant only takes messages until you choose a plan. Your data stays.

## Choosing or changing a plan

Only the business owner can choose or change the plan. Click **Choose** on a plan. VoiceDesk asks for your password, then opens Stripe's secure payment page. Card details are entered there and never reach VoiceDesk. Your plan changes as soon as Stripe confirms the payment, usually within seconds.

Once you have a plan, click **Manage billing** to open Stripe's billing page. There you can:

- move to another plan (Stripe works out the difference);
- update your card;
- download invoices;
- cancel. Your plan stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

## If a payment fails

Everything keeps working for 7 days, and a banner asks you to update your card in **Manage billing**. After 7 days the assistant only takes messages until the payment goes through.

## What Ask VoiceDesk does

[Ask VoiceDesk](/t/:tenant/ask) is an AI helper for you and your team, not for customers. Ask it about your bookings, calls, messages and customers, or ask it to do something. It looks through your own VoiceDesk data and answers in plain language.

## Example questions

- "What's booked for tomorrow?"
- "Which messages haven't been dealt with, and what do they want?"
- "Did anyone complain this week?"
- "Draft a reply to the WhatsApp customer waiting for us."

Type your question in the box (or click the microphone and say it) and click **Ask**. While it works you'll see "Looking into it…".

## Actions need your approval

Ask VoiceDesk never changes anything on its own. When you ask it to do something, it prepares a card marked **Waiting for your approval**. There are three kinds:

- **Reply**: a message to a customer conversation.
- **Cancel booking**.
- **Call back**: plans a call from the assistant to the customer.

On each card you can:

1. Click **Edit** to change a reply first (and **Cancel edit** to undo).
2. Click **Approve** to carry it out.
3. Click **Reject** if you don't want it.

The card then shows **Done**, **Rejected** or **Didn't work**. An action can fail if, for example, the booking was already cancelled, or the customer conversation can't be replied to any more.

## What it can see

It only sees what your role allows. Staff, admins and owners can all use it. Answers come only from your own business's VoiceDesk data.

## Privacy

Your questions are processed by the AI provider switched on for your VoiceDesk account. Don't paste in information that isn't already in VoiceDesk.

## If it can't answer

If you see "I couldn't find an answer to that.", try asking more specifically, for example with a date or a customer's name. Ask VoiceDesk needs an AI provider switched on; if none is, ask your VoiceDesk administrator.

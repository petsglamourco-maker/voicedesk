## What Contacts are

[Contacts](/t/:tenant/contacts) is your customer list. The assistant recognises callers by their phone number and greets returning customers with what it knows. It adds new people automatically when they book, sign up or leave a message.

Each contact shows where they came from: **Added by assistant**, **Imported** or **Added by hand**.

## Finding someone

Type a name, phone number or email into the **Search** box. The total number of contacts is shown under the list.

## Importing from a spreadsheet

You can bring in your existing customers from Excel, Google Sheets or your old system.

1. Save your list as a CSV file (under 3 MB, up to 5,000 rows).
2. Under **Import from a spreadsheet**, choose the **CSV file**.
3. VoiceDesk shows how many rows it found. For each column, choose what it is: **Name**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Notes**, **Agreed to marketing** or **Don't phone this person**. Choose **— not in file —** for anything missing.
4. Click **Import**.

You'll see how many were added, updated or skipped, with the line number and reason for any skipped rows. People already in your contacts are updated rather than duplicated.

Only import people you have a lawful reason to contact, such as existing customers. Marketing is only sent to people marked as agreeing to it.

## A contact's page

Click a contact to see their details and history:

- **Interested in**: what they asked about.
- **Calls and chats** across every channel.
- **Bookings**, **Complaints and issues** and **Messages**.

## Editing or deleting a contact

1. Open the contact and click **Edit**.
2. Change their name, phone, email or notes, and tick or untick **Agreed to marketing** and **Don't phone this person (they asked not to be called)**.
3. Click **Save**.

To remove someone, click **Delete**. Their calls, bookings and messages stay, but are no longer linked to them. This can't be undone.

## Don't phone

Contacts marked **Don't phone** are never called by the assistant, and the **Call** button is hidden for them.

## Calling a contact

The **Call** button opens the [VA console](/va) with the number ready to dial. Outbound calls only work once your VoiceDesk administrator has set up outbound calling, and they cost money at the phone provider.

## Who can use Contacts

Owners, admins and staff can view, import, edit and delete contacts.

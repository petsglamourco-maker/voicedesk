## Your first-day checklist

VoiceDesk gives your business an AI assistant that answers phone calls, website chat, WhatsApp, email and web forms. Work through these steps in order and you'll have a working assistant in about an hour.

1. Create your business.
2. Add knowledge (your website, documents, FAQs).
3. Generate and approve your business profile.
4. Choose a persona, voice and languages.
5. Test it in the Test console.
6. Connect your calendar.
7. Switch on your channels: website chat, WhatsApp, email, forms and phone.
8. Invite your team.

## Step 1 - Create your business

After you sign up and verify your email, you're taken to **Add your business**.

1. Enter the **Business name**.
2. Choose the **Country** (the United Kingdom for now; more countries are coming).
3. Check the **Time zone** and **Main language**.
4. Click **Create business**.

You can add another business later from the business switcher in the sidebar (**Add another business**).

## Step 2 - Add knowledge

Open [Knowledge](/t/:tenant/knowledge). Start with your website address, then upload price lists, menus or policy documents, and add any FAQs as text. The assistant reads these to understand your business.

## Step 3 - Generate and approve your business profile

Open [Business profile](/t/:tenant/profile) and click **Generate from knowledge**. Check every fact carefully (opening hours, prices, address, booking rules), fix anything wrong, then click **Approve & publish**. This step matters most: the assistant only uses the **approved** profile with customers. Until you approve one, it knows nothing about your business.

## Step 4 - Choose how your assistant sounds

On [Assistant](/t/:tenant/agent), pick a **Persona**, give it a name if you like, tick the **Languages** it should speak, and click **Save**. Use **Preview voice** to hear each one.

## Step 5 - Test it

Open [Test console](/t/:tenant/console). Pretend to be a customer: ask about prices, book an appointment, make a complaint. Try **Phone call (voice rules)** or **Talk by voice (microphone)** to check how it sounds on the phone. Bookings and messages made here are real records, so you can check the whole flow.

## Step 6 - Connect your calendar

On [Integrations](/t/:tenant/integrations), connect Google Calendar or Outlook / Microsoft 365, then choose the **Calendar for bookings**. The assistant then only offers times that are free in your diary.

## Step 7 - Switch on your channels

- [Website chat](/t/:tenant/website-chat): turn it on, add your website and paste the snippet.
- [WhatsApp](/t/:tenant/whatsapp): once your VoiceDesk administrator connects a number, share the link and QR code.
- [Email](/t/:tenant/email): connect your support mailbox with an app password.
- [Forms](/t/:tenant/forms): use the hosted enquiry form or connect your existing one.
- Phone: your VoiceDesk administrator assigns a number. It then appears on [Calls](/t/:tenant/calls), with codes to forward your existing number.

## Step 8 - Invite your team

On [Team](/t/:tenant/members), invite colleagues as **Admin** or **Staff**, and tick **Answers calls** for anyone who should take calls the assistant hands over.

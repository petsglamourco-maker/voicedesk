## About this page

[Assistant](/t/:tenant/agent) controls how your AI assistant sounds and behaves. It always tells customers it's an AI; that can't be switched off. Only owners and admins can change settings here; staff can view them. Click **Save** at the bottom after making changes.

## Persona and voice

Choose one of four personas. Each has its own tone and voice:

- **Amelia** (female voice): warm, calm and reassuring. Best for general reception (default).
- **James** (male voice): friendly, efficient and professional. Best for busy front desks.
- **Sofia** (female voice): bright and upbeat. Best for bookings and sales.
- **Daniel** (male voice): composed and empathetic. Best for complaints and sensitive calls.

Type a different name into **Name shown to customers** if you like. Choose a language next to **Preview voice** and click **Preview voice** on a persona to hear it.

## Languages

Tick the **Languages** the assistant may speak: English, Spanish, Mandarin Chinese, French, Arabic and German. It replies in the customer's language when that language is ticked. At least one must be ticked.

**Language for summaries and notifications** sets the language of call summaries and staff emails.

## Record calls

Switch on **Record calls** to keep a recording of phone calls. This adds a short recording notice to the greeting. Transcripts are always kept.

## Confirmations, reminders and alerts

- **Send customers a confirmation**: a short text (and an email, if they gave one) when a booking is made, moved or cancelled.
- **Appointment reminder**: **No reminder**, or a number of hours before.
- **Mobile for urgent alerts (optional)**: urgent problems and callback requests are always emailed to owners and admins; add a mobile to get a text too.

Texts cost money once your VoiceDesk administrator connects a text-message service.

## Putting callers through (phone calls)

When a caller asks for a person, the assistant can connect them instead of taking a message.

- **How calls reach VoiceDesk**: **VoiceDesk answers my number straight away**, or **My main line forwards to VoiceDesk when nobody answers**. With the backup option, callers hear that the team couldn't get to the phone, and the assistant books, arranges a call back or takes a message.
- **Ring the team first**: people who answer calls are rung (in VoiceDesk and on their phones) before the assistant helps. If nobody picks up, the assistant carries on. Included in the Growth and Pro plans.
- Each [department](/t/:tenant/departments) can have its own people, transfer number and email.

- **Who callers reach**: **VA console first, then the phone number**, **VA console only**, or **Phone number only**.
- **Seconds the VA console rings before giving up**.
- **Number to put callers through to** and **When** (**During opening hours** or **Any time**). Leave the number empty to always take a message instead.
- **Urgent situations (optional)**, such as "burst pipe, no heating", and an **Out-of-hours number for urgent situations**.

Life-threatening emergencies are always told to call 999. Transferred calls are charged by your phone provider.

## Follow-ups after enquiries

Switch on **Offer a follow-up when an enquiry doesn't book**. When someone asks about prices or availability and doesn't book, the assistant offers once to send them a message the next morning with times to book. It only sends one if they say yes.

- The message goes by text, email or WhatsApp, at 10:00 on your next open day. It says it's a one-off message and how to book (your phone number).
- If they book before then, nothing is sent.
- If they book within a week, the follow-up gets the credit. See **Follow-ups this month** on [Today](/t/:tenant/today) and on each call's page.
- Texts count against your plan's texts. Follow-ups are included in the Growth and Pro plans.

## Customer feedback

Choose whether customers are asked "How did we do?" after chats, WhatsApp, email and forms, whether callers get a rating text (costs money), and how often the NPS question is asked.

## Calls the assistant makes

Switch on **Call customers back when they ask for a callback** and **Phone to confirm bookings (instead of the reminder text)**, and set **Most calls per day**. Calls only go out between 8am and 9pm, never to customers marked "Don't phone", with at most two attempts. These need outbound calling set up and cost money.

## Daily brief

Switch on **Send the daily brief** for a 07:30 email to owners and admins. Click **Preview today's brief** to see it without sending anything.

## Workspace: voice typing

**Voice typing in text fields** shows a microphone in Ask VoiceDesk, Inbox replies, search and answer boxes, so your team can speak instead of type. It uses a tiny part of your AI allowance (about a penny a minute). Untick it to hide the microphone for everyone; each person can also hide it on their Account page.

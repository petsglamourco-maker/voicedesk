## Search or jump to anything

Press **Ctrl+K** (Windows) or **Cmd+K** (Mac) on any business page, or click **Search or jump to…** in the top bar. The palette lets you:

- go to any page, such as Inbox, Calls or Business profile;
- **Switch business** if you belong to more than one;
- change the **Appearance** (Dark, Light or Match my device);
- open the VA console, or Platform admin if you're the platform owner.

Start typing to filter, use the arrow keys to choose, and press Enter to go.

## Inbox shortcuts

On the [Inbox](/t/:tenant/inbox), when you're not typing in a box:

- **J**: next conversation.
- **K**: previous conversation.
- **R**: jump to the reply box.

## Closing things

Press **Esc** to close the search palette, menus and pop-up dialogs.

## Help

Press **?** to open Help, when you're not typing in a box.

## When a shortcut doesn't work

Single-letter shortcuts (J, K, R and ?) are ignored while your cursor is in a text box, so they never interrupt your typing. Click outside the box first, or press Tab to move away from it. On a phone or tablet without a keyboard, use the search button in the top bar and the menu button instead.

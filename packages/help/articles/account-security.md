## Your account page

Open **Account security** in the sidebar (under Settings) to reach [Account](/t/:tenant/account). Your account is yours, not the business's: the same sign-in works for every business you belong to.

## Changing your name

1. Under **Your name**, enter your **Full name**.
2. Click **Save name**.

Your name is shown to your team. When you take a call in the VA console, the caller hears your first name, for example "You're through to Sam now".

## Turning on two-step verification

Two-step verification asks for a code from an app on your phone when you sign in, so a stolen password isn't enough to get in. We strongly recommend it, especially for owners and admins.

You'll need an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.

1. Click **Turn on two-step verification**.
2. Enter your password under **Confirm your password**.
3. Add the key shown to your authenticator app (paste the link or enter it manually).
4. Save the **Backup codes** somewhere safe. Each can be used once if you lose your phone.
5. Enter the 6-digit code from the app and click **Finish setup**.

You'll see "Two-step verification is now on."

## Signing in with two-step verification

After your password, you're asked for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. If you've lost your phone, use one of your backup codes instead.

## Turning it off

Click **Turn off** and confirm your password. We don't recommend this.

## Owners and admins must use two-step verification

Business owners and admins have 7 days to turn it on (a reminder banner shows the date). After that, admin features stay locked until it's on; everyday features, and this Account page, keep working. The platform owner needs it before opening Platform admin.

## Changing your password

1. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter the **New password** twice (at least 10 characters).
3. Leave **Sign out on all my other devices** ticked unless you have a reason not to.
4. Click **Change password**.

Passwords that have appeared in known data breaches are refused, even if they're long. Only a tiny, anonymous part of a scrambled version of the password is checked; the password itself never leaves VoiceDesk.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email address and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email "Reset your VoiceDesk password" and follow the link. It works once and expires after 30 minutes.
4. Choose a new password and click **Save new password**.

Resetting signs you out on every device. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then ask Help "why aren't emails arriving?".

## Too many wrong passwords

After 10 wrong passwords, sign-in to that account is paused for 15 minutes, even with the right password, and we email you. This stops someone guessing your password. Wait 15 minutes, or reset your password with **Forgot password?**.

## New sign-in emails

When your account signs in from a browser or device it hasn't used before, we email you the browser, system, time and IP address. If it wasn't you, reset your password straight away and sign out the other devices below.

## Where you're signed in

**Where you're signed in** lists each browser or device with your account, when it was last used, and **This device** for the one you're using. Click **Sign out** next to any device you don't recognise, or **Sign out all other devices**. Then change your password.

You're signed out automatically after 7 days without using VoiceDesk.

## "Confirm it's you"

Some changes ask for your password again: anything in Platform admin, changing a team member's role, removing someone, and inviting an admin or owner. After you confirm, you won't be asked again for 5 minutes.

## Voice typing

Untick **Show the microphone in text fields** to hide the microphone button for you. Ask the help assistant about "voice typing" for how it works.

## Signing out and appearance

Click your picture at the top right to open the account menu. From there you can choose **Appearance** (**Dark**, **Light** or **Match my device**) and **Sign out**.

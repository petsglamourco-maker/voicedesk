## What Records shows

[Records](/t/:tenant/records) lists everything the assistant has done for customers, on every channel. Each item has a reference number that customers can quote.

## New customers signed up

People who signed up or enrolled through the assistant, with what they're interested in and whether they **Agreed to news and offers** or chose **No marketing**.

## Questions the assistant couldn't answer

These are things customers asked that weren't in your business profile or knowledge. Fixing them is the quickest way to improve your assistant.

Click **Add the answer** to open **Questions to answer** on the [Knowledge](/t/:tenant/knowledge) page. There, the same question asked in different words is grouped, with an answer drafted from your website and documents. Check it, edit it if needed, and click **Approve answer**. Use **Mark done** here for anything that doesn't need an answer.

Only owners and admins see these buttons.

## Complaints and issues

Tickets for complaints and problems, with their priority and the customer's callback number and preferred time. Change the **Status** to **Open**, **In progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed** as you deal with them.

## Messages for the team

Messages the assistant took, including callback requests, with who they're for. Mark each one **Done** when it's handled.

## Bookings

Every booking the assistant made, moved or cancelled, with its status (**Confirmed** or **Cancelled**).

To cancel a booking:

1. Click **Cancel** next to it.
2. Confirm. The booking is removed from your connected calendar and the customer gets a cancellation text.

## Assistant calls back

Next to tickets and messages with a callback number you may see **Assistant calls back**. Click it to have the assistant phone the customer back during calling hours (8am to 9pm) from your number. It then shows **Call planned**. This only works once outbound calling is set up, and calls cost money at your phone provider.

## Who can change records

Owners, admins and staff can update ticket status, mark messages done, cancel bookings and plan callbacks. Only owners and admins can add answers to the business profile.

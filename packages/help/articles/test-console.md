## What the Test console is for

The [Test console](/t/:tenant/console) lets you talk to your assistant exactly as a customer would. Use it after every change to your profile, knowledge or assistant settings. Owners, admins and staff can all use it.

Records it creates here are real, so you can check the whole flow: a booking made here appears in [Records](/t/:tenant/records) and your connected calendar. Cancel test bookings afterwards.

If you see a warning that no business profile is approved, approve one first. Otherwise the assistant knows nothing about your business.

## Starting a test

1. Choose **Start in**: the language to begin with.
2. Choose a **Style**:
   - **Website chat**: a typed chat.
   - **Phone call (voice rules)**: typed, but the assistant answers the way it would on a phone call.
   - **Talk by voice (microphone)**: a real voice call in your browser.
3. Optionally enter a **Simulated caller ID** to test how the assistant recognises a known contact.
4. Click **Start conversation**.

## Chatting

Type as a customer in the message box and click **Send**. Each reply shows how long it took and roughly what it cost.

The side panel shows:

- **Engine**, **Flow** and **Language**: what the assistant thinks the customer wants.
- **Tool calls**: actions it took, such as checking availability or creating a booking.
- Records created, such as a **Booking**, **Ticket**, **Message**, **Callback request** or **Sign-up**.

## Talking by voice

With **Talk by voice (microphone)**, allow microphone access when your browser asks. You'll see a live transcript and the response time of each reply. Click **Hang up** to finish. If the assistant doesn't join, voice isn't set up on this VoiceDesk server yet; ask your administrator.

## Ending a test

Click **End & summarise** to close the conversation and see the **Summary** your team would get. Click **Start new conversation** to try again.

## Ideas for tests

- Ask about prices and opening hours.
- Book, move and cancel an appointment.
- Make a complaint or ask for a person.
- Switch language mid-conversation.
- Ask something not in your profile, then check **Questions the assistant couldn't answer** in Records.

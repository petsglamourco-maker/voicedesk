## What the VA console is

The [VA console](/va) lets you answer phone calls in your browser when a caller asks the assistant for a person. You see the conversation so far on screen, so you don't have to ask the caller to repeat themselves. It also lists chats waiting for a person.

Open it with the headset icon at the top right of any business page, or from the account menu. It opens in a new tab.

## Before calls can ring for you

You must be set up to answer calls for at least one business:

- a business owner or admin ticks **Answers calls** for you on their Team page; or
- your VoiceDesk administrator assigns you as a VA.

Otherwise you'll see "You don't answer calls for any business yet". The top of the console shows **Answering for** and the businesses you cover.

## Being available

1. Set **Status** to **Available**.
2. Keep the tab open. Leaving it, or closing the laptop, makes you offline within a minute.
3. Browsers only play sound after you've clicked something, and choosing your status is that click. If the console opened already showing Available, choose **Available** again so you'll hear the ringtone.

Other statuses are **Busy**, **Away** and **Offline**. Calls only ring while you're Available. When nobody answers, calls go to the business's backup number or the assistant takes a message.

## Answering a call

Waiting calls appear under **Calls** with the business name, why they called, how long they've been **waiting**, and an **Urgent** badge where needed. The first person to click **Answer** gets the call; the others stop ringing.

The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone. Choose Allow. A headset gives the best sound.

During the call you'll see the **Call time**, the live transcript, **Records from this call** and **Notes**. You can:

- **Mute** or **Unmute** yourself;
- **Open contact** to see the customer's history;
- **Hand back to assistant** when you're done but the caller may still need something. The assistant tells them they're back with the AI assistant and carries on;
- **Hang up** to end the call.

## Chats waiting for a person

**Chats waiting for a person** lists website chat, WhatsApp, email and form conversations where the customer asked for a person or the assistant needs help. Open one to reply from the Inbox.

## Calling a customer

**Call a customer** lets you phone someone, showing the business's number. Choose **Calling for** (the business), enter the **Number**, tick **They asked us to call back** if they did, and click **Call**. This only works once outbound calling is set up, it costs money at the phone provider, and unsolicited calls are only allowed between 8am and 9pm. Outbound calls aren't transcribed or recorded yet, so note anything important on the contact.

## What WhatsApp does

Customers message your business on WhatsApp and the assistant answers, in the same way as on the phone and website. Every WhatsApp conversation appears in the [Inbox](/t/:tenant/inbox), where you can take over and reply yourself.

## Getting a WhatsApp number

If [WhatsApp](/t/:tenant/whatsapp) says **No WhatsApp number yet**, your VoiceDesk administrator needs to connect a WhatsApp Business number for you. You can't do this from the business pages. Once it's connected, the page shows **Your WhatsApp number**.

## Sharing your WhatsApp

Once your number is connected you get two ways to share it:

- **Click-to-chat link**: put this on your website, in emails and on your social profiles. It opens a WhatsApp chat with your business.
- **QR code**: print it on flyers, menus or your shop window. Scanning it with a phone camera opens a chat with you.

## The 24-hour rule

WhatsApp only lets businesses reply freely within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, the customer needs to message you first. If you need to reach someone after 24 hours, call them or use another channel.

## Ratings on WhatsApp

When a conversation ends, the assistant may ask "How did we do?" and the customer can reply with a number from 1 to 5. Results appear on [Feedback](/t/:tenant/feedback).

## Costs

Replying to customers who message you first is normally free. Messages your business starts (outside the 24-hour window) are charged by Meta, and VoiceDesk doesn't send those at the moment.

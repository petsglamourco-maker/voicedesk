## Your numbers

[Phone numbers](/t/:tenant/numbers) lists your assistant's numbers and how many your plan includes. Starter and Growth include 1 number; Pro includes 2.

## Getting your number

Your own number comes with a paid plan. On the free trial, try the assistant in the test console, or forward your existing number to it.

1. Choose **Local** (an area code, like 023) or **Mobile** (07…, which can also send texts).
2. The first time, send your business details: registered name, company number, website, a contact and your business address. Phone companies check every business before giving it a number; it usually takes 1–3 working days.
3. Search by area code or town, pick a number and click **Get this number**.
4. Once the check is done, the number is set up automatically within a few minutes and starts answering calls.

If someone else takes the number you picked before it's set up, we give you the nearest free number in the same area.

## Extra numbers

Need a second line, for another shop, a sales line or another area? Click **Add extra number**: local numbers are £5 a month and mobile numbers £8 a month (or the same in your currency), added to your plan's bill from that day. Give one up any time with **Give up number**; the charge stops and the number goes back to the phone company (you can't get it back).

## Keeping your existing number

You can keep the number your customers already know: forward it to your assistant's number, all the time or only when you're busy or don't answer.

## When a plan ends

If your plan stops, your numbers stop answering. They're kept for 30 days, and come back if you restart your plan; after that they're released.

## What Help can do

The Help assistant answers questions about using VoiceDesk: how a screen works, how to set something up, and what to check when something isn't working. It suggests questions for the page you're on, and links you straight to the right screen.

Open Help with the round button in the bottom-right corner, by pressing **?** (when you're not typing in a box), or by choosing **Help & support** in the search palette (**Ctrl+K**, or **Cmd+K** on a Mac). Press **Esc** to close it. Your chat stays while you move between pages; **New chat** starts again.

## Where its answers come from

Help answers from the VoiceDesk help articles. It can also check simple yes-or-no facts about your setup to give you a more useful answer, for example whether a business profile has been approved, whether a calendar is connected, or whether website chat is turned on.

If the articles don't cover your question, Help says so rather than guessing.

## What Help can't see

Help can't see your customers' data: no names, phone numbers, conversations, recordings or bookings. It also can't change any settings for you; it tells you where to go and what to click.

Please don't paste customers' personal details into Help. Describe the problem instead, for example "a booking made by phone this morning isn't in my calendar".

## Contacting support

If Help can't solve your problem, click **Contact support** (under the chat, or on an answer). Write what you need and click **Send to support**. Your message goes to the VoiceDesk team with the page you were on, and they reply to you by email. Tick **Include this help conversation** if you want them to see your chat with Help; otherwise only your message is sent. Check your spam folder if you don't see a reply.

Never send passwords, API keys or two-step verification codes to support. The VoiceDesk team will never ask for them.

## Rating answers

Each answer has a thumbs up and a thumbs down. Thumbs down shows a **Contact support** button, so you can ask a person straight away. The thumbs stay in your browser; to tell the VoiceDesk team an answer was wrong, use **Contact support**.

## Tips for good answers

- Ask one question at a time.
- Use the names you see on screen, such as "Business profile" or "Test console".
- Say what you expected and what happened instead.

## Emergency stop

[Spending & limits](/platform/spending) has three switches that apply to every business at once:

- **Pause all outbound calls**: the assistant's callbacks and confirmation calls, and people phoning customers. Incoming calls still work.
- **Pause all texts**: confirmations, reminders and alert texts. Emails still go.
- **Pause AI (take messages only)**: the assistant takes messages without AI on every channel.

Each change is recorded in the audit log, and asks for your password if you haven't confirmed it in the last 5 minutes.

## Suspending a business

**This month by business** lists every business's spend against its own limits. Click **Suspend** to stop a business straight away: its assistant only says the service isn't available, and its texts and calls stop. Its team can still sign in and see their data. Click **Resume** to turn it back on.

## Plans

Each business's monthly allowance comes from its plan (Starter, Growth or Pro, or the 14-day free trial). The **Plan** column shows it. Use **Change…** to give a business a plan free of charge, for example for a pilot customer, or to restart its 14-day trial. Each change is recorded in the audit log.

## Countries and cost estimates

- **Let businesses set their own limits** (off by default): when on, owners can also choose lower limits within their allowance on their Usage & limits page.
- **Countries**: where calls and texts may go. Premium-rate and special-rate numbers are always blocked.
- **Cost estimates**: what a voice minute, a phone-line minute and a text cost, and the dollar-to-pound rate (AI costs are recorded in dollars). Update them from your provider bills so the spend figures stay accurate.

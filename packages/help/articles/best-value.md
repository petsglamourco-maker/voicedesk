## Keep your business profile up to date

The assistant only states facts from your **approved** business profile. When your prices, opening hours, staff or offers change, update [Business profile](/t/:tenant/profile) and click **Approve & publish** again. Add seasonal hours and bank holidays under **Notes (bank holidays, seasonal hours…)**, and current promotions under **Current offers**.

Fill in the **Sales: turning enquiries into customers** section too. It tells the assistant what a successful conversation ends in (a booking, a sign-up or either) and why customers choose you, so it can recommend the right service and get people booked.

## Fix the questions it couldn't answer

[Records](/t/:tenant/records) has a list called **Questions the assistant couldn't answer**. Check it once a week:

1. Click **Add the answer** to open the business profile, and add the answer as an FAQ.
2. Click **Approve & publish**.
3. Back on Records, click **Mark done**.

Each answer you add means one less call or message that needs a person.

## Keep knowledge fresh

Websites are re-checked weekly, but if you change your site or a document, open [Knowledge](/t/:tenant/knowledge) and click **Refresh** on that source. Use **Test what the assistant finds** to check a question finds the right passage.

## Listen to your customers

- [Feedback](/t/:tenant/feedback) shows how customers rate conversations. Read the comments on low scores; you're also alerted to them straight away.
- The [Dashboard](/t/:tenant) shows how customers felt, what they wanted and when they get in touch. Use the busiest times to plan staffing.

## Use the daily brief

Switch on **Send the daily brief** on [Assistant](/t/:tenant/agent). Every morning at 07:30 owners and admins get one email with yesterday's activity, today's bookings and anything waiting.

## Set up handoff routing

Under **Putting callers through (phone calls)** on [Assistant](/t/:tenant/agent), choose **Who callers reach**. The default, **VA console first, then the phone number**, gives callers a real person whenever someone is available, and falls back to a phone number or a message. Tick **Answers calls** on [Team](/t/:tenant/members) for the people who should take them.

## Test regularly

After any change to the profile, knowledge or assistant settings, spend five minutes in [Test console](/t/:tenant/console). Try a booking, a complaint and a question you've just added.

## Let Ask VoiceDesk do the routine work

[Ask VoiceDesk](/t/:tenant/ask) answers questions about your own data and prepares actions for you to approve. Useful examples:

- "What's booked for tomorrow?"
- "Which messages haven't been dealt with, and what do they want?"
- "Draft a reply to the WhatsApp customer waiting for us."
- "Cancel the booking for the customer who called this morning."

Nothing happens until you click **Approve**.

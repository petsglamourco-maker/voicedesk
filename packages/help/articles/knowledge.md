## What Knowledge is

[Knowledge](/t/:tenant/knowledge) is what your assistant knows about the business: your website, documents and FAQs. VoiceDesk reads them and uses them to draft your business profile and to answer customers' questions.

Owners and admins can add and remove knowledge. Staff can view it and use the search test.

## Adding your website

1. Under **Add knowledge**, choose the **Website** tab.
2. Enter your **Website address**.
3. Set **Max pages** (how many pages to read).
4. Click **Add**.

VoiceDesk reads the public pages on your site, respecting robots.txt, and re-checks them weekly. Only add websites you own or have permission to use.

## Uploading a file

1. Choose the **File** tab.
2. Choose a PDF, Word (DOCX), CSV or text file, up to 20 MB.
3. Click **Add**.

Good files to add: price lists, menus, policies, terms and conditions, service descriptions.

Files are checked when you upload them: a file whose contents don't match its type (for example a program renamed to .pdf) is refused. Very large documents are refused too: PDFs over 500 pages, and Word files that are huge once unpacked. Split them into smaller files.

## Adding text or FAQs

1. Choose the **Text / FAQ** tab.
2. Enter a **Title** and the **Text**.
3. Click **Add**.

This is handy for things that aren't written down anywhere else, like parking tips or what to bring to a first appointment.

## Your sources

Everything you add appears under **Sources**, with how many pages and passages were read. The status shows **Queued**, **Reading…**, **Ready** or **Failed**.

- **Refresh** reads the source again, for example after you update your website.
- **Delete** removes it.

If a website shows **Failed**, check the address opens in your browser and that the site is public.

## Testing what the assistant finds

Under **Test what the assistant finds**, type a question a customer might ask (for example "Do you have parking?") and click **Search**. You'll see which passages the assistant would use. If you see "Nothing relevant found.", add that information.

## Knowledge and the business profile

When a customer asks something, the assistant checks your approved [Business profile](/t/:tenant/profile) first and searches your knowledge when the answer isn't there. Key facts such as hours, prices, address and booking rules belong in the profile. After adding new knowledge, use **Generate from knowledge** there, check the draft and approve it.

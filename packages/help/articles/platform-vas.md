## What VAs are

VAs (virtual assistants) are people who answer calls handed over by the AI assistant, across several businesses. Businesses can also let their own members answer calls, using **Answers calls** on their Team page.

**Platform admin → VAs** lists everyone who answers calls and the businesses they cover. A business's own members are marked **own staff**.

## Assigning a VA

1. Make sure the person has a VoiceDesk account (they sign up with their own email first).
2. Under **Assign a VA to a business**, enter **Their account email**.
3. Choose the **Business**.
4. Click **Assign**.

Repeat for each business they should cover.

## What assigning does

Assigning makes them staff of that business, so they can see its calls, chats and contacts in the VA console and the business pages. They appear on the business's Team page marked **VA**.

## Removing a VA

Click **Remove from** next to the business. They stop receiving that business's calls and lose access to it.

## How calls reach VAs

1. The business sets **Who callers reach** under **Putting callers through (phone calls)** on its Assistant page.
2. The VA opens the [VA console](/va), sets **Available** and keeps the tab open.
3. When a caller asks for a person, every available answerer for that business rings. The first to click **Answer** gets the call.

Answering calls in the browser is free; only the phone call itself costs money at the carrier.

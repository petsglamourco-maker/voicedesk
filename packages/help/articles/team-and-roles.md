## The Team page

[Team](/t/:tenant/members) lists the people who can sign in to manage this business, and any pending invitations. Only owners and admins can manage the team; everyone else sees "Only owners and admins can manage the team."

## Inviting someone

1. Under **Invite a team member**, enter their **Email address**.
2. Choose a **Role**.
3. Click **Send invitation**.

They get an email with a link. They sign in or create an account with that same email address, then click **Accept invitation**. Until then they're listed under **Pending invitations**, where you can **Cancel** the invitation.

## The three roles

- **Owner**: can do everything, including inviting other owners.
- **Admin**: can do everything except the owner-only actions. Admins can invite admins and staff, but not owners, and can't change an owner's role.
- **Staff**: day-to-day work. Staff can view the Dashboard, Inbox, Calls, Contacts, Records, Knowledge and Business profile; reply in the Inbox; edit and import contacts; update records and cancel bookings; use Ask VoiceDesk and the Test console. They can't change the assistant, business profile, knowledge, channels, integrations or team.

## Changing a role or removing someone

Use the role menu next to a member to change their role, or click **Remove** to take away their access. You can't change your own role or remove yourself here. These changes, and inviting an admin or owner, ask you to confirm your password first (once every 5 minutes).

## Letting someone answer calls

When a caller asks to speak to a person, the assistant can ring people at the [VA console](/va).

1. Find the person in **Members**.
2. Tick **Answers calls**.

They then open the VA console, set themselves to **Available** and keep the tab open. Choose how calls are routed under **Putting callers through (phone calls)** on [Assistant](/t/:tenant/agent).

## VAs

People marked **VA** are virtual assistants assigned by your VoiceDesk administrator. They answer handed-off calls for you and have staff access to your business.

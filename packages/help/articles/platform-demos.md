## What instant demos are

The public **/try** page lets anyone type in their website and, in about a minute, talk to an AI receptionist built from it, by voice in the browser or by chat. It's the quickest way to show a potential customer what VoiceDesk does for _their_ business.

Each demo:

- reads up to 10 pages of the website and builds a business profile from them;
- allows 20 chat messages and 3 voice calls of up to 3 minutes;
- can never send a text, make a call or email anyone;
- is deleted, with everything said in it, after 24 hours.

## Switching demos on

Demos are off until you switch them on, because each one costs a few pence of AI and voice.

1. Open **Platform admin → Demos**.
2. Tick **Allow instant demos on /try**.
3. Set **Demos a day**: the most demos all visitors together can start in a day. Start small (for example 5).
4. Click **Save** and confirm your password.

An AI provider must be switched on under **AI providers**, or /try says demos aren't available. Each visitor can start at most 3 demos an hour. With the bot check set up (Turnstile), robots are turned away too.

## What it costs

The page shows today's count and this month's AI spend for demos. A demo usually costs about 5–15p, mostly voice and building the profile. Each demo's cost appears in the list once it has been deleted.

## Leads

Visitors can leave their email, with a tick to agree that VoiceDesk may email them about the demo. Those emails appear in the list. They're kept for 90 days and then cleared automatically. If someone asks you to delete theirs sooner, click **Forget email**.

## Privacy

/try tells visitors that their demo is deleted after 24 hours and that what they say is processed by your AI providers. That may be outside the UK, including DeepSeek in China when it's switched on. If you change providers, check that the notice is still true.

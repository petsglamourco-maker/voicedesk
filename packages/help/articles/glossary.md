## The assistant

- **Assistant**: your AI receptionist. It answers phone calls, website chat, WhatsApp, email and forms for your business, and always says it's an AI.
- **Persona**: the assistant's personality and voice (Amelia, James, Sofia or Daniel). You can rename it on the Assistant page.
- **Knowledge**: your website, documents and FAQs that the assistant reads and searches.
- **Business profile**: the checked list of facts (hours, prices, address, policies, FAQs) the assistant can state with confidence. Only the **approved** version is used.
- **Draft**: an unpublished version of the business profile, or an email reply waiting for your approval.
- **Flow**: the kind of conversation the assistant is handling, such as an enquiry, booking, complaint, reschedule, cancellation or message.

## Channels

- **Channel** or **touchpoint**: a way customers reach you: phone calls, calls by the assistant, website chat, WhatsApp, email and forms.
- **Website chat** or **widget**: the chat button on your website.
- **Snippet**: the short piece of code you paste into your website to show the chat.
- **Hosted form**: a ready-made enquiry form VoiceDesk provides at its own link.
- **Webhook**: an address your existing form tool sends enquiries to.

## People and handoffs

- **Owner**, **Admin**, **Staff**: the three team roles. Owners can do everything; admins nearly everything; staff do day-to-day work.
- **VA** (virtual assistant): a person who answers calls handed over by the assistant, sometimes for several businesses.
- **VA console**: the browser page where people answer handed-over calls and see chats waiting for a person.
- **Handoff**: when the assistant passes a caller to a person, in the VA console or by transferring to a phone number.
- **Transfer**: putting a caller through to an ordinary phone number.
- **Take over**: a person replies in an Inbox conversation instead of the assistant.
- **Hand back**: returning a call or chat to the assistant.
- **Presence**: your VA console status: Available, Busy, Away or Offline.

## Records

- **Booking**: an appointment the assistant made, with a reference.
- **Ticket**: a complaint or problem to deal with.
- **Message**: a note for the team, often a callback request.
- **Sign-up**: a new customer who joined or enrolled.
- **Knowledge gap**: a question the assistant couldn't answer, shown on Records.
- **Reference**: the short code customers can quote for a booking or ticket.

## Feedback and numbers

- **CSAT** (customer satisfaction): the share of customers who rated a conversation 4 or 5 out of 5.
- **NPS** (Net Promoter Score): from "Would you recommend us?" (0 to 10), promoters (9 to 10) minus detractors (0 to 6).
- **Response time** or **latency**: on calls, how long the assistant takes to start answering after the caller stops speaking.
- **Median**: the middle value; half the replies were faster.
- **Daily brief**: the 07:30 summary email for owners and admins.

## Tools

- **Test console**: where you talk to your assistant as a customer would, to test it.
- **Ask VoiceDesk**: an AI helper for your team that answers questions about your data and prepares actions for you to approve.
- **Command palette**: the search box opened with Ctrl+K or Cmd+K.

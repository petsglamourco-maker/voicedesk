## What this page is for

**Platform admin → Countries** decides which countries businesses can sign up in. The UK is always open. Ireland, the United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand are built in and wait for you to open them.

Each country has its own rules built in:

- the emergency number the assistant gives callers (999, 911, 000, 111…);
- whether call recording needs everyone's agreement;
- the hours the assistant may phone customers;
- do-not-call lists and marketing consent;
- currency and local time zones;
- blocked premium-rate numbers.

## Opening a country

1. Have the country's rules checked by a qualified adviser there. The built-in rules are sensible defaults, not legal advice.
2. Tick **Open for sign-ups** next to the country and click **Save**, then confirm your password.
3. New businesses can now choose that country when they add their business, and get its time zones, currency and rules.

You'll also need phone numbers in that country from Twilio or Telnyx, which ask for local documents.

## US texting

Business texts to US numbers only reach people after carrier registration (called A2P 10DLC) at Twilio or Telnyx. Until you tick **Texting registration done**, VoiceDesk doesn't send texts to US numbers. Confirmations go by email instead.

## Calls the assistant makes

In the US, Canada and Australia, the assistant only phones customers who asked for a callback. Booking-confirmation calls stay off there until the rules have been reviewed.

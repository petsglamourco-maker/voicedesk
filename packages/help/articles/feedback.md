## What the Feedback page shows

[Feedback](/t/:tenant/feedback) shows how customers rate their conversations with your assistant and your team. Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days** at the top.

- **Satisfied (CSAT)**: the share of ratings that were 4 or 5 out of 5, with the average score.
- **Net Promoter Score**: promoters (9 to 10) minus detractors (0 to 6).
- **Responses** and **Response rate**: how many customers answered out of those asked.
- **By channel**: scores for website chat, WhatsApp, email, forms and calls.
- **Who looked after them**: the **Assistant** or **A person**.
- **Latest ratings**, with any comments.

## How customers are asked

At the end of a conversation the customer gets one quick question: "How did we do?" (1 to 5).

- **Website chat**: rating buttons appear after the goodbye.
- **WhatsApp**: the question is added to the goodbye, and the customer replies with a number.
- **Email and form enquiries**: an email with a rating link.
- **Phone calls**: a text with a rating link, only if you switch it on (texts cost money).

Nobody is asked more than once a month. The wording is fixed and translated into each language.

## Low scores

Low ratings alert owners and admins straight away, so you can follow up while it still matters.

## Changing the settings

Owners and admins can change these under **Customer feedback** on [Assistant](/t/:tenant/agent):

- Turn the rating question on or off for chats, WhatsApp, email and forms (free).
- **Also text callers a rating link after phone calls (texts cost money)**.
- **"Would you recommend us?" (NPS) question**: **Never**, or every 5th or 10th survey.

Click **Save** when you're done.

## A note on rating links

Rating links in emails and texts open a VoiceDesk page. They only work for customers once VoiceDesk is hosted on a public web address. The buttons in website chat and replies in WhatsApp work without that.

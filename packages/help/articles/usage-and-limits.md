## What this page shows

Open **Usage & limits** in the sidebar (under Settings) to reach [Usage & limits](/t/:tenant/usage). It shows this month's spend in three parts:

- **AI**: the cost of the assistant's answers, summaries, Ask VoiceDesk and the Help assistant.
- **Texts**: confirmation, reminder and alert texts sent through a real phone carrier.
- **Calls**: minutes on the phone (incoming and outgoing) and browser voice calls.

Each part has a progress bar against your monthly allowance: what your plan includes each month. Months follow your business's time zone. The amounts are estimates based on VoiceDesk's provider costs.

## Your allowance

Your allowance comes from your plan. You can use all of it; VoiceDesk doesn't limit anything your plan covers. If you regularly run out, contact VoiceDesk support for a bigger allowance. Once billing is available, you'll be able to move to a bigger plan.

If VoiceDesk has switched on **own limits**, the owner can also choose lower limits within the allowance under **Your monthly limits**, and change them whenever they like. Set a limit to 0 to switch that kind of spending off.

## What happens when the allowance is used up

- **AI**: the assistant doesn't go silent. It takes messages instead: it asks for the customer's name, number and message, and saves it for your team in Records. Ask VoiceDesk and AI summaries pause.
- **Texts**: confirmations, reminders and alert texts stop. Emails still go.
- **Calls**: the assistant's callbacks and confirmation calls, and people phoning customers, stop. Incoming calls are still answered, so customers can always reach you.

Owners and admins get an email at 80% and again at 100%. Everything starts again on the 1st of next month, or straight away when the allowance is raised.

## Where calls and texts can go

To protect against fraud, calls and texts only go to allowed countries (the UK by default). Premium-rate and special-rate numbers (for example 09, 087, 084 and 070 numbers) are always blocked. This also applies to the transfer, on-call and alert numbers in [Assistant settings](/t/:tenant/agent).
